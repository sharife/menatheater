Thank you for your interest in becoming a MENA Theater Alliance member!

We have four categories of membership:

  • MENA Organizational Membership $75–$500 / year
  • MENA Individual Membership $50 / year
  • Ally Organizational Membership $100–$750 / year
  • Ally Individual Membership $75 / year

Please choose a membership based on the descriptions below.

MENA Organizations and Individuals

Benefits

  • Member profile on our site
  • Access to the MENA Theater Maker and Cultural Worker database
  • Opportunities listings in e-blasts, social media, and our online forum
  • Show promotions in e-blasts and social media
  • Discounts on MENATMA events and offerings

MENA Organizational Membership $75–$500 / yr

This membership category is open to organizations that have MENA representation in their leadership, staff, and collaborators, as well as a history of arts and cultural work that is deeply connected to, and has positively impacted, MENA artists and audiences in their community.

Benefits

  • Show promotions for MENA-centered productions
  • Discounts on public MENATMA events and offerings
