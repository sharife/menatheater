#### MENA Theater Makers: We Can Make the Home We've Been Looking For

*American Theatre Magazine* published the full text of Board President Denmo Ibrahim's moving welcome speech at our 2026 Fall Convening — with an intro by MENATMA Board Member and Golden Thread Productions Founding Artistic Director Torange Yeghiazarian.

> “I wonder if our generation is being asked to do something different. To dismantle some of those binaries. To find the courage to live in the spaces between. To become comfortable with discomfort, of not fitting neatly into one category or another. To recognize that transition itself can be a place to stand on one’s own two feet.”
